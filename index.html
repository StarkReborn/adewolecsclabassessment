Short biography

I am an undergraduate student of Computer Science at Miva Open University. My interest is in the seam between design and engineering: understanding what a product needs to say, then building the small, reliable pieces of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript that let it say that clearly. I write frontend code in React and TypeScript, prototype in Tailwind, and spend an unusual amount of time reading specifications.

The projects on this site are presented as they are — student work, described modestly, without invented users, metrics, or awards. Where something is unfinished, I say so.
