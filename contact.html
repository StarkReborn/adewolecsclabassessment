Write a note — the form
is honest about its limits.

This page validates your input thoroughly, then tells you the truth: this is a static site, so nothing is actually delivered yet. Below is exactly what the form checks.

Send a message

Must look like name@domain.tld.

Digits only. No spaces, no +, no punctuation, no letters. Example: 08012345678.

At least 10 characters. Aim for a paragraph or two.

Validation passed

Your input passed every check. Nothing has been sent — this is a static demonstration. To actually receive messages, a form endpoint must be configured by the site owner.

The integration point is documented in js/contact.js as FORM_ENDPOINT.

Validation rules in full

  1. No field may be empty after trimming whitespace.
  2. Email must match a sensible format: letters@letters.tld.
  3. Phone must contain digits only. A leading +, spaces, or punctuation are rejected.
  4. Message must contain at least 10 characters.

On invalid submit, focus moves to the first invalid field. Errors are announced via role="alert". No page reload is required.
