A little about me

Curious by nature,
building by choice.

I'm Victor, a Computer Science student, frontend developer, and technology enthusiast from Lagos, Nigeria. I enjoy exploring ideas, solving problems, and turning things I imagine into something people can actually use.

My story

My name is Adejokun Victor Adewole, although many people know me online as StarkReborn. I've always been drawn to understanding how things work, questioning what is possible, and finding ways to bring ideas to life.

That curiosity eventually led me into web development. What started as an interest in how websites are built gradually became a passion for creating digital experiences. I enjoy the entire process, from imagining what something could look like to writing the code that makes it work.

Over time, I've had opportunities to work on websites for businesses and organisations, collaborate with other people, and take projects from ideas to working products. Those experiences have helped me understand that development involves much more than writing code. It requires creativity, patience, communication, and a willingness to keep learning.

I'm still early in my journey, and there is a lot I want to explore. That's one of the things I find exciting about technology: there is always something new to understand, build, or improve.

Education

I'm currently pursuing a B.Sc. in Computer Science at Miva Open University, Nigeria.

Studying Computer Science gives me the opportunity to understand the foundations behind the technology I work with. I'm particularly interested in programming, computational thinking, problem-solving, and the principles that make software work.

I enjoy connecting what I learn academically with practical projects. Being able to apply an idea outside the classroom makes the learning experience even more rewarding.

Where I'm headed

Frontend development is where much of my experience currently lies. I enjoy creating interfaces that are thoughtful, intuitive, and enjoyable to use. I'm particularly interested in the relationship between design, functionality, and the experience people have with technology.

My interests extend beyond web development. Artificial intelligence has become an increasingly important area of exploration for me. I'm fascinated by how AI could transform industries, expand access to knowledge, and help people solve problems that once seemed difficult or impossible.

In the long term, I want to grow into an engineer with a broader understanding of technology, someone who can work across software engineering and emerging technologies to build meaningful solutions.

I have big ambitions, and I'm committed to developing the knowledge, discipline, and experience required to pursue them.

A few things I believe

I believe curiosity is one of the most valuable things a person can have. It makes you ask better questions, look beyond what already exists, and imagine what could be.

I don't have everything figured out, and I don't expect to. What matters to me is remaining willing to learn, experiment, make mistakes, and keep improving.

I want to build things that matter, work with people who challenge me to think bigger, and contribute to a future where technology creates more possibilities for everyone.
